**United Software Engineers** is the IT club of Mukhtar Auezov University students. We came together to build real projects, not just hand in lab assignments.

## What we'll do

- **Team projects** — websites, bots, apps. With tasks, code reviews and releases, like in a real company.
- **Hackathons** — we prepare and compete together.
- **Internships** — we help students get internships at IT companies in Kazakhstan.

## How to join

Message our Telegram bot — the link is on the “How to join” page. No experience required.
